This policy explains what personal information InterviewAlpha collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain. If anything is unclear, email us at [contact email].

Who we are

InterviewAlpha (https://interviewalpha.app) is run by Tom Griffiths, a sole trader based in the UK, trading as InterviewAlpha. For UK data protection law, Tom Griffiths trading as InterviewAlpha is the "controller" of your personal information. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean InterviewAlpha.

  • Email: [contact email]
  • Postal address: [postal address]
  • ICO registration number: [ICO registration number]

The short version

  • Your workspace is private. We use your information to run InterviewAlpha for you, not to sell it or show you adverts.
  • We don't apply to jobs for you and we don't share your CVs or applications with recruiters or employers.
  • You can export your data and delete your account at any time from your Account page.
  • We use only the cookies needed to sign you in and run the site. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

What we collect

Account details. Your email address, which you use to sign in with a magic link (we don't use passwords), and basic account records such as when you signed up and last signed in.

What you add to your workspace. Applications (company, role, stage, outcomes, job board, dates, next steps, notes and interview details), CV Version records (labels, change notes and file details) and the CV files you upload (PDF, DOC or DOCX, up to 4 MB each). This can include information about other people, such as a recruiter's name, if you choose to add it.

Payment details (Paid plan only). The Paid plan is sold to you by Stripe, through its Link service, as merchant of record. You enter your card details on Stripe's secure pages, never on ours. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe tells us only what we need to run your plan: a Stripe customer reference, your subscription status, renewal date, whether a payment succeeded or failed, and whether a payment has been disputed.

Help assistant questions. If you use Ask InterviewAlpha, the help assistant, we handle your questions as described in our separate notice, How Ask InterviewAlpha uses your questions. In short: it doesn't see your account, we don't send it your CVs, applications, notes, contacts or emails, and we don't save your chat.

Technical information. When you use the site, our hosting and database providers automatically log technical details such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and error information. We use these to keep the service secure and working. To stop misuse of the help assistant, we keep short-lived counters linked to a scrambled (hashed) version of your account ID or IP address, never the text you typed.

Messages you send us. If you email us, we keep your message and our reply so we can help you.

The demo. The interactive demo at /demo needs no account. Changes you make are held in cookies in your browser and briefly in our server's memory, and are reset within 24 hours. Demo data is never saved to our account database. If you add a CV in the demo, only the file name is remembered; the document itself is not uploaded.

Information you choose to include

CVs and notes can contain more than you need for job tracking. Please don't add sensitive details, such as health information, ethnicity, religion or political views, unless you are happy for us to store them. We don't need them and we don't use them for anything except storing them for you. If you add information about other people (for example a referee's contact details), please only add what you need and make sure you are comfortable doing so.

Why we use your information, and our legal basis

UK data protection law requires us to have a legal reason (a "lawful basis") for each use.

What we do Lawful basis
Create and run your account, sign you in, store and sync your workspace and CV files, show your Insights Contract (to provide the service you asked for)
Manage your Paid plan (checking whether it is active, showing your renewal date) Contract
Keep any records the law requires us to keep Legal obligation
Send essential emails, such as sign-in links, notices about your plan and important changes to the service or these terms Contract, and legitimate interests in keeping you informed
Keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems Legitimate interests (running a safe, reliable service)
Answer help assistant questions Legitimate interests (helping you use the product)
Reply to your emails and handle complaints or legal claims Legitimate interests
Any optional extras, such as product news emails Consent (we will ask first, and you can withdraw at any time)

We don't currently send marketing emails. We don't use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Insights are simple statistics about your own applications, shown only to you.

We never sell your personal information.

International transfers

Some of our providers are based in, or store data in, the United States or other countries outside the UK. When your information is transferred outside the UK, we make sure it is protected by an approved safeguard, such as a UK adequacy decision (including the UK-US Data Bridge where the provider is certified), the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for more detail.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and workspace: for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your account, applications, notes, CV Version records, every uploaded CV file and our record of your Paid plan.
  • [PROPOSED] Deleting your account while on Paid: you can choose either:

Stripe and Link keep their own payment and tax records under their own policies. - Technical logs: short periods, normally no more than [30] days. - Help assistant rate-limit counters: up to 24 hours. - Emails you send us: up to [2] years after we last hear from you, unless we need them longer for a complaint or legal claim. - Inactive accounts: we don't currently delete inactive accounts automatically. If we decide to, we will email you well in advance.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your personal information (you can also download it yourself from your Account page);
  • correct anything inaccurate (you can edit most of it yourself in the app);
  • delete it (you can delete your account yourself from your Account page);
  • take it with you (data portability: CSV and JSON exports are on your Account page, and you can download each CV file from CV Versions);
  • object to uses based on legitimate interests;
  • restrict how we use it in some circumstances;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email [contact email]. We will reply within one month. We may need to confirm it's you, for example by replying from the email address on your account.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first at [contact email] so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ or 0303 123 1113.

Security

Your workspace is protected so that only you can access it when signed in. CV files are stored privately. Information is encrypted in transit. No online service can be completely secure, so please keep access to your email account safe, as that is how you sign in.
