2. Billing questions (FAQ lines for the Account page and help)

  • What will I see on my card statement? A charge starting LINK.COM* (for example LINK.COM* [descriptor]). Link is Stripe's payment service, which sells InterviewAlpha Paid for us.
  • Who sends my receipt? Link, by email, each month. You can also see receipts in Manage billing or at link.com.
  • Is tax included? Yes. $14.99/month includes tax. Stripe, as the seller (merchant of record), handles the tax.
  • If I cancel, do I get a refund? No refund for part months. You keep Paid until your next billing date. In your first 14 days you can cancel for a refund, minus a proportionate amount for the days you've used.
