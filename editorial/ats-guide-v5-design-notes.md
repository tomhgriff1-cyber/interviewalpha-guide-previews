# ATS guide v5, internal design notes

This note is for the editorial handoff. It is not part of the reader-facing guide.

## What Tom asked for

A magazine or publication page: interesting type, clear space, and graphics in the InterviewAlpha brand. The v4 diagrams did not do that. He also asked to stop repeating country disclaimers in the body. Keep a local difference only when it changes a practical choice, and keep who a source is in a short source note.

## Visual references

These published template previews were used as principles only. They are copyrighted. None of the preview images were copied, embedded, or traced.

- Living, a magazine template on GraphicRiver. Tom reported 601 product sales. That is the only sales figure treated as verified here. Useful ideas: a strong opening, a grid, changes of scale, and wide paper margins. https://graphicriver.net/item/magazine-template-a4-and-letter-living/9403993
- Wildnun, an editorial ebook template on Creative Market. Design reference only, not a claimed bestseller. Useful ideas: a large serif, italic, warm paper, and charcoal contrast. https://creativemarket.com/wildnunstudio/292345212-Editorial-Ebook-Templates-for-Canva
- Provo, an editorial workbook template on Creative Market. Design reference only. Useful ideas: asymmetry, small running labels, fine rules, and whitespace. https://creativemarket.com/studioprovo/91890115-Editorial-Workbook-Template-Canva
- SIMON, an editorial workbook on Creative Market. Design reference only. Useful ideas: sage with charcoal, an oversized serif, and a clear hierarchy. https://creativemarket.com/AESTETHIC_STUDIO/291588756-SIMON-Editorial-Aesthetic-Workbook

## Brand and type

Public InterviewAlpha CSS uses paper `#F5F2EA`, forest `#214D3C`, ink `#172D26`, pale green `#E8F0EB`, muted `#64716A`, and rules `#D9DDD3`. The live site names Charter, Heros, and Latin Modern. Heros is a commercial family, and the live font files were not copied. This preview self-hosts Source Serif 4 and Source Sans 3, both under the SIL Open Font License, with the licence texts in `ats-guide-v1/fonts/`. Headings and body are the serif. Labels are the sans.

The cover artwork is an original SVG: warm paper, a forest plane, a cropped page of rules, and a large letter used as a shape. It does not depict software and it does not reuse a template.

## Copy

Repeated lines such as "that is not a map of every product or every country" were removed from the essay. Length, photographs, personal details, references, and the words CV and resume stay in "What to include and leave out", next to the official source that states them. The source list still says who each organisation is.
