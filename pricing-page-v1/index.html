Questions

Is there a free trial?

The Free plan is the way to try InterviewAlpha. It has no time limit and no card is needed: you get 5 applications in total and up to 2 CV Versions at a time, with the Command Centre, Board, Insights and CV Versions all included. You can also explore the demo without an account.

How do I cancel Paid?

From your Account page, any time. Your plan won't renew, and you keep Paid until your next billing date. After that your account moves to Free and nothing is deleted.

Can I get a refund?

We don't refund part months when you cancel. If you're a consumer in the UK, you have a legal right to cancel your first Paid purchase within 14 days of buying it. Because Paid starts straight away, you'll be refunded what you paid minus a proportionate amount for the days you had Paid. Stripe, as merchant of record, may also issue refunds under its own terms. None of this affects your statutory rights. All the details are in the Terms of Service (sections 6 and 7).

What currency will I pay in?

The price is $14.99/month including tax. At checkout, Stripe may show you the price in your local currency, and the amount you pay is always shown before you confirm.

What happens to my data if I go back to Free?

It all stays. You can view, edit and export every application and CV Version. Free includes 5 applications in total (open or closed) and up to 2 CV Versions at a time, so if you're above those limits you can't add new ones until you upgrade again (for CV Versions, you can also remove some to get back within the limit).

Who processes my payment?

Stripe, through its Link service, is the merchant of record: it sells Paid on our behalf, takes your payment, handles tax and sends your receipts. Your card statement shows LINK.COM* INTERVIEWALPHA. You can update your card and see receipts from Manage billing on your Account page. See our Privacy Policy for how payment information is handled.
