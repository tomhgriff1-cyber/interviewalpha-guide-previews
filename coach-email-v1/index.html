Why career coaches and CV writers? They work with job seekers every day, and their clients are exactly the people InterviewAlpha is for. One coach who likes the tool can mention it to many clients, so a small number of friendly emails can go further than advertising. Coaches also give honest feedback, which is useful this early.

A coach trying the demo, signing up free, or saying they'd mention it to a client is a great result. No replies after a week is also useful information: we change the angle, not the volume.

4. UK law note (general information, not legal advice)

Cold email in the UK is mainly covered by PECR (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations) and UK GDPR. In plain words:

  • Limited companies and LLPs are "corporate subscribers". The PECR consent rule for marketing email does not apply to them, so you can send them a business email without prior consent. You must still say clearly who you are and give a working way to opt out, and you should honour opt-outs and keep a "do not email" list.
  • Sole traders and ordinary partnerships are treated like individuals. Unsolicited marketing email to them generally needs their prior consent (or the "soft opt-in", which only applies to your existing customers). So we should not cold email them.
  • UK GDPR still applies when an email address identifies a person (for example firstname@company.co.uk), even at a company. That is why this pack uses generic addresses (info@, hello@, enquiries@) and contact forms, not named staff emails. If you ever do email a named person, you need a lawful basis (usually legitimate interests) and must tell them where you got their details if they ask, and stop if they object.
  • If you can't tell whether a business is a company or a sole trader, the ICO says to treat it as an individual. That is why the "Unknown" rows say "check entity first".

Recommendation: email limited companies via their published generic address. For sole traders, either skip them for now or use their contact form with a short, plain, non-marketing note (for example, asking if they would be open to hearing about a tool for their clients) and only follow up if they say yes. In week one, stick to the limited companies.
