## 1. What this is for

This is a draft for your approval. Nothing has been sent and nobody has been contacted.

Why career coaches and CV writers? They work with job seekers every day, and their clients are exactly the people InterviewAlpha is for. One coach who likes the tool can mention it to many clients, so a small number of friendly emails can go further than advertising. Coaches also give honest feedback, which is useful this early.

How many to send at first: 5 to 10 in week one. Send them by hand, one at a time, from support@interviewalpha.app. A small batch means you can read every reply properly, fix anything that isn't landing, and you avoid looking like bulk mail (which hurts deliverability).

What counts as success: replies. Any reply is good, even "not for us", because it tells you the message was read. For 10 cold emails, 1 or 2 replies is a normal, decent result. A coach trying the demo, signing up free, or saying they'd mention it to a client is a great result. No replies after a week is also useful information: we change the angle, not the volume.

Each email below is a different angle. Pick the one you like most (or one per small batch, to compare). The coach list after that shows who to contact, how they publish their contact details, and whether they look like a limited company, which matters for UK email rules (see the law note).

## 4. UK law note (general information, not legal advice)

- Limited companies and LLPs are corporate subscribers: PECR's consent rule for marketing email does not apply, but you must identify yourself and give an opt-out, and honour it.
- Sole traders and ordinary partnerships are treated like individuals: unsolicited marketing email generally needs prior consent (or soft opt-in for existing customers).
- UK GDPR still applies to named staff emails, so use generic addresses or contact forms.
- If unsure of entity type, treat as an individual.
- Recommendation: email limited companies via generic addresses; for sole traders, skip or use their contact form with a short non-marketing note.
- ICO: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/ | https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/electronic-and-telephone-marketing/electronic-mail-marketing/ | https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-direct-marketing-using-electronic-mail/how-do-we-comply-with-the-pecr-electronic-mail-marketing-rules
