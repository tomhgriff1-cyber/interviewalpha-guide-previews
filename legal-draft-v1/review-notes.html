Legal review notes and open items

Prepared: 23 October 2026 · Status: draft for Tom's approval and solicitor review. Nothing here is published on interviewalpha.app.

A. Needs a solicitor or verification

  1. Trader name and geographic address. The Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 (Schedule 2) and the Electronic Commerce Regulations 2002 (reg 6) require the trader's identity, a geographic address and an email address before a distance contract. Tom's name therefore appears in "Who we are" in both documents and nowhere else. A PO box is not enough; a virtual office or registered service address may be. Placeholders: [contact email], [postal address].
  2. Stripe Managed Payments / merchant of record not yet verified. The payment and tax clause (Terms s5) and the Stripe note in the Privacy Policy are written to work either way. Once confirmed, check: who the consumer contracts with for the purchase, who handles refunds and the 14-day cancellation, whether Stripe's checkout shows its own consumer terms, and whether Stripe is an independent controller. If Stripe is not merchant of record, InterviewAlpha must deal with UK VAT registration thresholds, EU VAT on digital services to EU consumers (from the first sale, via the non-Union OSS) and US sales tax.
  3. 14-day cancellation right. Drafted on the basis that Full is a digital service (not digital content), so under the CCR the consumer who asks for immediate start keeps the right to cancel but pays a proportionate amount. Confirm the classification, the express-request checkbox mechanism (our pre-checkout page vs Stripe's consent box), and the confirmation email on a durable medium.
  4. DMCC Act 2024 subscription rules apply from 1 January 2027 to consumer subscriptions entered into on or after that date (draft Consumer Subscription Contracts (Cancellation and Information) Regulations 2026 and guidance published 19 October 2026). Expect: key pre-contract information presented in a set way, reminder notices, easy online cancellation, a renewal cooling-off right and new refund rules. Review the Checkout copy, cancellation flow and Terms s5 to s7 against the final guidance before January.
  5. Price display. $14.99 in US dollars to UK consumers. UK price transparency rules (DMCC Act, in force April 2025) require the total price including mandatory charges and VAT to be shown up front. If tax is added at Stripe Checkout, "$14.99" on our pages may be treated as drip pricing. Decide tax-inclusive pricing or show the total; consider GBP for UK users.
  6. "Unlimited" with fair use. No fair-use number exists in the repo for applications or CV Versions (only help-assistant rate limits and a 4 MB per-file CV limit). Fair use is defined generically. Advertising rules (CAP Code) only allow "unlimited" where a fair-use policy affects very few, moderate users; check marketing wording.
  7. Age: 18+ drafted. 16+ is lawful for UK GDPR consent purposes (13+ for online services) but 18+ avoids minors' contract capacity issues. Decide.
  8. International transfers. Confirm each provider's region and transfer mechanism: Supabase project region (docs suggest EU), Vercel function region, Upstash store in iad1 (US East), Resend, Stripe, OpenAI. Confirm DPAs are accepted and whether each relies on the UK-US Data Bridge, IDTA or UK Addendum.
  9. Special category and third-party data. CVs and notes may contain health or other sensitive details, and information about recruiters and referees. The policy asks users not to add sensitive data. A solicitor should confirm the Article 9 position and whether InterviewAlpha is controller or processor for third-party data users enter.
  10. ICO data protection fee. Most sole traders processing personal data must pay the ICO fee. Placeholder: [ICO registration number].
  11. Retention defaults are proposals. Backups [7] days (check Supabase plan), logs [30] days (check Vercel plan), emails [2] years, billing records about 6 years (HMRC requires at least 5 years after the 31 January filing deadline for sole traders).
  12. Liability cap. Greater of £100 or 12 months' fees. Check this is fair under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
  13. Transfer to a successor company. Drafted with notice and a "no reduction in your rights" promise to avoid an unfair term. Under UK GDPR the new company becomes controller; notice and an updated policy will be needed.
  14. Scotland and Northern Ireland. England and Wales law with local court and mandatory-law carve-out. Confirm.
  15. Help assistant lawful basis. Legitimate interests used, consistent with the published Ask InterviewAlpha notice. Confirm.

B. Product facts in the repo that conflict with, or add to, the brief

  1. Upstash Redis is an extra processor. The help assistant rate limiter uses Upstash via the Vercel Marketplace (store interviewalpha-assistant-ratelimit, region iad1, US). Identifiers are HMAC-hashed, no text stored, keys expire with their window (up to 1 day). Added to the processor list.
  2. The demo is not purely browser-local. On Vercel, demo changes live in gzipped httpOnly cookies (ia_db_*) plus server memory, and reset after 24 hours (DEMO_OVERLAY_MAX_AGE_SEC). The demoMode cookie lasts 30 days. Demo data never reaches Supabase and demo CV files aren't uploaded (filename only). The policy describes this accurately.
  3. Removing a CV Version is a soft delete. userDeleteCV sets deleted_at; the record and stored file stay (and appear in the JSON export as removed versions) until the account is deleted. The policy says so. Consider adding a "permanently delete this file" option.
  4. Users cannot delete individual applications. Every application counts towards the Free limit of 5, including Rejected and Withdrawn (DB trigger ia_enforce_free_application_limit). After a downgrade a user with more than 5 applications can never get back under the limit without upgrading. Copy reflects this (CV Versions can be removed to free a slot; applications can't).
  5. Resend may not be live yet. Docs say sign-in emails go through Supabase Auth, with Resend as the intended custom SMTP. If Resend isn't configured at launch, Supabase is the email sender; the processor list still holds either way.
  6. Ask InterviewAlpha is shipped but disabled in Production (ASSISTANT_ENABLED unset). The policy refers to it conditionally ("if you use").
  7. No billing code exists yet. plans.ts says paid is "coming soon" and the Free-limit DB triggers assume every account is Free; they need to become plan-aware before Full launches.
  8. Feature promise on the sign-up page. signup-tiers.tsx lists "Longer history and filtering on Insights" for Full. Confirm it exists before charging, or remove it. The Terms don't promise it.
  9. Deleting the account mid-period. The brief says access continues to the end of the paid period on deletion, but deletion is immediate and irreversible in the current flow. Drafted as: deletion cancels the subscription immediately with no refund for the rest of the month, and suggests cancelling first and deleting later. Alternative: schedule deletion for the period end.
  10. No legal links anywhere yet. The landing page and app have no footer links to /privacy or /terms; these need adding along with the pages. The export excludes CV files (users download each from CV Versions), which the policy reflects.
  11. No analytics. No analytics or tracking package or script is in the repo, so the cookie section lists essential cookies only (sb-*-auth-token, demoMode, viewingDemo, ia_db_*, ia_help_sid, ia_help_rl).
