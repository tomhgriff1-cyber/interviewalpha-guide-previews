2. Upgrade button and pre-checkout summary

Shown on our page before sending the user to Stripe Checkout.

Button (Account page, Free limit messages): Upgrade to Full

Summary panel title: Upgrade to Full

Summary body:

  • Full plan: $14.99 a month [tax wording: "including VAT" / "plus any applicable tax, shown before you pay"]
  • Unlimited applications and CV Versions (fair use applies)
  • Billed today, then monthly on the [day] of each month until you cancel
  • Cancel any time from your Account page. You keep Full until the end of the month you've paid for. We don't refund part months.
  • You have 14 days to change your mind. If you cancel in that time, we'll refund you, minus a proportionate amount for the days you've had Full.

Consent checkbox (required, unticked by default):

[ ] I want Full to start straight away. I understand that if I cancel within 14 days, I'll get a refund minus a proportionate amount for the days I've used it.

Terms checkbox or line (required):

[ ] I agree to the Terms of Service and have read the Privacy Policy.

Primary button: Continue to secure payment
Under button: You'll pay on a secure Stripe page. Payments are processed by Stripe.

If using Stripe Checkout's own consent box instead (consent_collection.terms_of_service with custom_text.terms_of_service_acceptance), combine into one line:

I agree to the Terms and want Full to start straight away. If I cancel within 14 days, I'll get a refund minus a proportionate amount for the days I've used it.

Stripe Checkout custom_text.submit (above the Pay button):

$14.99 a month until you cancel. Cancel any time in your InterviewAlpha Account page.

4. Cancellation confirmation dialog

Standard (more than 14 days after first purchase)

Title: Cancel your Full plan?
Body: Your plan won't renew. You'll keep Full until [end date], the end of the month you've paid for. We don't refund part months.
After [end date] your account moves to Free. Everything you've added stays, and you can still view, update and export it. You'll only be able to add new applications or CV Versions while you're within the Free limits (5 applications, 2 CV Versions).
Buttons: Cancel plan (destructive style) · Keep Full

Within 14 days of first purchase

Title: Cancel your Full plan?
Body: You're within 14 days of starting Full, so you can cancel now for a refund of $[amount], which is what you paid minus a proportionate amount for the [n] days you've had Full. Your account will move to Free straight away. Nothing is deleted.
Buttons: Cancel and refund (destructive style) · Keep Full
Text link: Or cancel at the end of the month instead, with no refund

5. Post-cancel banner

Shown until the period ends

Your Full plan is cancelled. You have Full until [end date], then your account moves to Free. Nothing will be deleted. [Keep Full]

Shown once after moving to Free

You're now on the Free plan. All your applications and CV Versions are still here, and you can view, update and export them. [Upgrade to Full]

After a 14-day refund

Your Full plan is cancelled and a refund of $[amount] is on its way. It can take 5 to 10 working days to reach your account. You're now on the Free plan.

6. Downgrade and over-limit messages

Banner when over the Free limits

Title: You're over the Free limits
Body: You have [n] applications and [n] CV Versions. Free includes 5 and 2. Your data is safe: you can still view, update and export everything. To add new ones, upgrade to Full.

You've reached the Free limit of 2 CV Versions. Remove one, or [upgrade to Full] to add more. Your existing CV Versions are safe.
