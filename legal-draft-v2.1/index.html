InterviewAlpha legal drafts (v2.1)

Draft Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and pricing and cancellation microcopy, v2.1, prepared 23 October 2026 for Tom Griffiths to approve. Not published on interviewalpha.app. Placeholders in square brackets still need filling. v2 was approved by Tom, pending the solicitor items. Earlier versions remain at v1 and v2 for comparison.

What changed in v2.1

  • Price shown everywhere as $14.99/month including tax (your local currency may be shown at checkout).
  • Statement descriptor set to LINK.COM* INTERVIEWALPHA.
  • Deleting while on Paid is confirmed (no longer marked as proposed): delete at the end of the paid period, or delete everything now.
  • New: deleted data is recoverable for 30 days, then permanently purged; "permanently delete now" skips the window for erasure requests.
  • GitHub added as a processor (weekly encrypted backup, US transfer); backup retention [30] days.
  • Free: up to 2 CV Versions at a time, plus 5 applications in total.
  • Stripe may issue refunds or settle disputes under its own terms; statutory rights unaffected.
What changed in v2
  • Plan renamed from "Full" to Paid: unlimited, with a fair-use cap of 1,000 applications and 50 CV Versions.
  • Price shown only as $14.99/month including tax, monthly.
  • Stripe (through Link) is the merchant of record and handles tax; the "we are the seller" fallback is removed. Statements show LINK.COM* and receipts come from Link.
  • Cancelling keeps Paid until the next billing date, with a worked example. Disputes don't downgrade automatically.
  • Proposed two ways to delete while on Paid: at the end of the paid period, or everything now with no refund.
  • Free described as a way to try InterviewAlpha: 5 applications in total and 2 CV Versions, with clear, kind over-limit messages.
  • 18+ removed (no age limit stated); Insights promise removed; review notes updated.
